The AI Search Gap

Since May 2026, Google has been rolling AI agents into search results, and that rollout keeps expanding through 2027. Instead of scanning a list of results, more customers now ask a question and get a direct answer from an AI agent, built from whatever data Google can pull about each business.

Most local Business Profiles were never built for this. Hours, a category, maybe a handful of reviews, and nothing else. No FAQ section. No structured pricing logic. No answers to the specific questions your actual customers ask before they call. When the agent has nothing to draw from, it either says nothing about you or defers to whichever competitor's profile does have answers.

What We Actually Build

We start with an audit of what Google's AI agent currently has access to on your profile, most of the time it's close to nothing. From there we pull real questions, either from an owner interview or from your actual call history if you have it, so the knowledge base reflects what people genuinely ask, not a generic template. We structure that into FAQs, pricing logic, and service details, and upload it directly to your GBP, along with conversation starters and a welcome message so the agent has something natural to open with. After that, it's monitored and refined monthly as new questions come in.

Common Questions

AI Knowledge Base Training FAQ

What exactly is Google doing with AI agents in search?
Since May 2026, Google has been rolling AI-powered agents into search results that answer customer questions directly, pulling from Business Profile data instead of just returning a list of links. It's an active rollout, not a future maybe.
How is this different from SEO?
SEO gets you found. This makes sure that once the AI agent finds you, it actually has something accurate to say. A profile that ranks well but has a blank FAQ section still leaves the agent with nothing to answer with.
Do I need a lot of existing reviews or content for this to work?
No. This runs off structured data, FAQs, pricing logic, service details, not review volume. A brand new profile and a ten-year profile both start from the same first step: an audit of what the agent currently has access to.
How long does setup take?
The audit, owner interview, structuring, and upload happen as a one-time project. After that, the monthly work is refinement as new questions surface, not a rebuild each month.
Does this replace Google Reviews Automation or Missed Call Text-Back?
No, it runs alongside them. Reviews and structured FAQs feed different parts of what Google's AI agent can say about you. This one is aimed specifically at the FAQ and knowledge gap.
Is this proven yet?
This is a new service, built in direct response to Google's AI rollout. We're not going to pretend it has years of track record, because it doesn't, Google's agents don't either. What we can tell you honestly is what the audit finds on your specific profile, and what a filled-in one looks like next to it.
