Common Questions

Quick answers before you call.

What is a log home?

A log home has full log walls. The logs go on horizontally and are the finished exterior wall. Interior walls are usually conventionally framed, or can be full log if you want.

What is a timber frame home?

A timber frame home has a decorative and structural wood skeleton held together with timber pegs or fasteners. The exterior is finished with the siding of your choice, and the timbers stand out inside.

What is a post and beam home?

A post and beam home is mostly conventionally framed, with wood posts and beams added as accents. It is the least expensive way to get wood accents in your home.

What is a hybrid timber home?

A hybrid timber home mixes timber frame and full log features with conventional framing and finishes.

Do you offer dry-in only service?

Yes. Tolbert Contracting provides turnkey builds and dry-in only services for log and timber frame kits and packages.
