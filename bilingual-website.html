Bilingual Website Add-Ons

Two ways to put your site in Spanish. Pick the one that fits how your customers find you.

Add-on for any website client Full Spanish version We set it up and keep it updated
Who It's For

Owners who speak Spanish, or whose customers do.

If you or your customers read Spanish first, your site should too.

Option 1

ES/EN Toggle

One page. Every section in English and Spanish. A language button in the menu switches between them, and the site remembers the choice on the next visit.

  • Spanish-speaking customers who already have your link
  • An owner who wants to read their own site in Spanish

The button: ES / EN (Español / English)

What it does not do: it does not help you show up in Spanish Google searches. Search engines see one page. For Spanish search, you need the /es/ page.
Option 2

Spanish Page at Its Own Address (/es/)

A full Spanish page at yoursite.com/es/. It has its own title, description, headings, FAQ and language tag, and it is paired with your English page so Google knows which one to show each searcher.

  • Built for people who search in Spanish
  • A Spanish link you can send and share on its own
The honest part: this makes your page eligible to rank in Spanish searches. No guarantee of rankings or customers.
Which One

Which one do I need?

  • Customers already have your link and some read Spanish: the toggle.
  • You want people searching in Spanish to find you: the /es/ page.
  • Not sure: call or text and we'll sort it out.
Common Questions

Bilingual Website FAQ

Will the toggle help me show up in Spanish searches?
No. It's for people who already have your link. For Spanish search, you need the /es/ page.
Will the /es/ page bring me Spanish-speaking customers?
It makes your page eligible to show up in Spanish searches. Rankings depend on Google and your competition. We don't guarantee rankings or customers.
Can I add this to my site?
Yes. Both are add-ons for any Platypus website client.
What happens when I need a change?
Send it to us. We update the English and the Spanish together. Occasional edits are handled the same way as the rest of your site.
Who checks the Spanish?
We write the Spanish version and send it to you to read before it goes live. If nobody on your side reads Spanish, we can arrange a paid proofread first.
