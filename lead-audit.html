What Is the Lead Audit?

The Lead Audit is a free AI-powered diagnostic tool that evaluates your lead response process, identifies where you're losing revenue, and shows you exactly what's costing you money every month.

How It Works

Answer 6 simple questions about your business:

  • Lead volume and frequency
  • Response time to customer inquiries
  • Current lead capture methods
  • Your biggest operational bottleneck
  • Annual revenue range
  • Timeline for improvement

Our AI analyzes your answers against industry benchmarks and delivers a personalized diagnosis showing:

  • How much revenue you're likely losing monthly
  • Where your response gaps are vs. competitors
  • Which customers you're missing
  • What a fix would generate in new revenue

Industry Benchmarks

Your audit compares your business against real industry standards for your trade. See exactly how you stack up against the competition and what fast responders are doing differently.

Vertical-Specific

We have specialized knowledge bases for 14 service verticals: HVAC, Plumbing, Roofing, Electrical, Tree Service, and more. Each one uses industry-specific questions and benchmarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About the Lead Audit

How long does the audit take?
About 2 minutes. The audit is 6 simple multiple-choice questions, followed by an instant personalized diagnosis.
Is the audit really free?
Yes, completely free. No credit card, no email spam list, no sales pressure. We give you the diagnosis. If you want to talk about solutions, we'll offer, but it's 100% optional.
What if my industry isn't on the list?
We have 14 service verticals: HVAC, Plumbing, Roofing, Electrical, Painting, Tree Service, Junk Removal, Foundation Repair, Paving, Concrete, Hardscapes, Crawlspace Services, Septic Services, and Land Clearing/Grading. If your trade is on that list, we have industry-specific questions and benchmarks. If not, you can still take a general version.
How accurate are the benchmarks?
Our benchmarks come from published industry data, real client results, and aggregate performance trends across service verticals. They're directional, not absolutes. The goal is to show you where you stand relative to the field, not to be perfect down to the dollar.
What happens after the audit?
You get a diagnosis immediately. If it resonates and you want to explore solutions, you can book a discovery call with our team. No obligation. Most owners use the diagnosis to make their own decisions.
Will you sell my data?
No. We use your answers to generate your personalized diagnosis and optionally send you a summary email if you request it. That's it. We don't share or sell contact info.
