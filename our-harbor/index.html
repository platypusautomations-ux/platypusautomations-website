About Us

Why Our Harbor exists

Our Harbor is a community outreach non-profit built for teens, started by Becky. It brings together mentoring, tutoring, faith, advocacy, and just plain fun, all under one roof, so teens have a real place to land. Echo is the name of the teen program itself: the part of Our Harbor built to feel like it belongs to them.
